# 03 - Epics & User Stories

## Epic 1: Structural Evaluation & Insurance Claim Reconciliation

- **US-01**:
  - **As a** Civil Engineer, Architect, Topographer, or UDGR Inspector
  - **I want** to log property type (`residential` or `commercial`), occupant role (`owner` or `tenant`), photo evidence, damage inventory, technical damage level (`superficial`, `chronic`, `structural`), and UDGR habitability verdict (`safe`, `atRisk`, `uninhabitable`)
  - **So that** insurance companies and banks reconcile claims fast without families losing their homes or savings
  - **In the context of** post-earthquake field inspections across Cali neighborhoods and commercial zones
  - **Must have the format** of a simple Private Inspector form that produces a traffic-light badge (Green Safe, Yellow At Risk, Red Uninhabitable) and reconciled claim plan.

## Epic 2: Humanitarian Triage & Ambulance Dispatch

- **US-02**:
  - **As a** UDGR Emergency Health Coordinator
  - **I want** to classify affected citizens by age (`<10`, `>10`, `>=18`, `>=60`), care conditions (`hasDisability`, `isVulnerable`, `hasSpecialNeeds`), and population group (`civilian`, `military`, `prison`)
  - **So that** ambulances and priority rescue teams reach critical individuals first
  - **In the context of** over-saturated hospitals and emergency evacuations after the earthquake
  - **Must have the format** of a quick triage form that outputs a sorted Red-Yellow-Green priority queue with clear ambulance flags.

## Epic 3: Public Business Intelligence (BI) & Citizen Inquiry

- **US-03**:
  - **As a** Cali Citizen or General Public Member
  - **I want** to view live traffic-light statistics for all 6 city sectors (`economy`, `health`, `infrastructure`, `education`, `logistics`, `housing`) and search my property by name or address
  - **So that** I can understand city conditions and check my building's habitability and insurance claim progress
  - **In the context of** public transparency during post-earthquake recovery in Cali
  - **Must have the format** of a child-friendly Public BI Dashboard tab with big counters, color lights, and an instant search box.
